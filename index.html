Hair Restoration

Hair transplant: The solution to your hair fall problems

Thinning hair and hair loss are common conditions many men and women encounter when they enter their third decade of life. It can result from aging, trauma, or a medical condition.

The condition progresses rapidly if no suitable treatment is undertaken, leading to severe hair loss.

Various treatment methods are available to manage hair loss, including medicines, laser therapy, PRP, and hair transplant. Amongst all, hair transplant, especially direct hair transplant, is a gold standard treatment for hair loss with long-lasting results.

Cosmo International, one of the leading hair transplant clinics in Hyderabad, India, offers high-quality hair transplant treatments.

When should you get a hair transplant?

Patients should consider hair transplant when they have turned 20 or older, and both men and women can undergo the treatment.

At Cosmo International, we recommend hair transplants to patients whose hair loss has stabilized. You can gain more valuable insights into hair loss experienced at any age from our certified surgeons. They can guide you on the best course of action and treatment for your situation.

How does it work?

Direct hair transplant (DHT) includes the removal of hair follicles from the donor area (sections with dense hair growth). These follicles are then implanted one by one into the area that has been affected.

Each hair follicle is placed in a specific direction and depth, providing 100% natural-looking hair. The implanted hair continues to grow like natural hair throughout the person’s life.

Our highly trained and experienced surgeons perform the procedure following the quality protocol that we have set up to ensure our clients get great results.
