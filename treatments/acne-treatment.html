Frequently Asked Questions

What causes acne?

Acne is primarily caused by excess oil production, clogged pores, bacteria, and inflammation. Hormonal changes, stress, and diet can also contribute to acne flare-ups, especially during puberty or in individuals with oily skin.

How long does acne treatment take to show results?

Results vary depending on the severity of acne and the treatment method. Typically, noticeable improvements can be seen within 4 to 6 weeks of consistent treatment, though some cases may take longer.

Is acne treatment safe?

Yes, we provide the best acne treatments in Hyderabad and it is generally safe when prescribed or recommended by a professional. However, it's important to follow the treatment guidelines and discuss any concerns or allergies with your healthcare provider.

Can acne be prevented?

While acne can't always be fully prevented, maintaining a proper skincare routine, managing stress, eating a balanced diet, and avoiding harsh skincare products can reduce the frequency and severity of breakouts.

Are acne treatments suitable for all skin types?

Yes, acne treatments in Hyderabad can be tailored to all skin types. Different types of acne may require specific treatments, so it’s important to consult with a professional to find the best option for your skin.
