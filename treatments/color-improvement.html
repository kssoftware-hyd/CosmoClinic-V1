Overview

Each time you leave your home, you expose yourself to pollution, sun, bacteria, and other harmful components. These elements make your skin rough, dry, dull, and dark. You might start looking older than your actual age, which can hurt your confidence. Fortunately, there is a solution to solve this problem.

Nowadays, several color improvement treatments are available like carbon facial, chemical peels, Glutathione injections, and microdermabrasion to restore your youthful and glowing skin. Cosmo International offers all these color improvement solutions to help you get rejuvenated, brighter, and fresh skin by treating concerns like age spots, pigmentation, sun tan, and dark spots.

Carbon Facial

Dust, pollution, and an unhealthy lifestyle can make your skin look dull, rough, and dehydrated. The carbon facial is an effective two-step, instant glow, non-invasive treatment for clear and rejuvenated skin. A carbon layer is applied on the skin’s surface that removes all the dirt from the clogged pores. Then a focused light beam is administered that peels the mask off, along with the dead skin, dirt, and oil, to reveal smooth, clear, and soft skin. The treatment deeply exfoliates the skin and stimulates collagen production to treat all skin woes like hyperpigmentation, sun damage, and premature aging.

Chemical Peels

It is painless and is among the best treatments for wrinkles, discolored skin, and scars. Our cosmetologists clean your skin and apply a chemical solution to the area where discoloration occurs. Leave it to dry and peel it off to reveal a smooth and fresh layer of skin. Cosmo International offers light, medium, and deep chemical peels to treat various skin conditions.

Glutathione Injections

Glutathione is an amino acid antioxidant that whitens the skin and treats liver toxicity. It helps neutralize the free radicals in the body to eliminate impurities and reduce hyperpigmentation, leaving you with brighter and glowing skin. Our expert cosmetologists administer Glutathione injections to reduce cosmetic defects, correct uneven skin tone, and brighten the complexion.

Microdermabrasion

Microdermabrasion is a minimally invasive procedure to renew overall skin tone and texture. The process uses a special machine that applies a slightly rough applicator tip to the skin’s surface, removing the uppermost layer. As a result, it improves the appearance of skin affected by age spots, sun spots, acne scars, wrinkles, and hyperpigmentation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is color improvement treatment?

Color improvement treatments in Hyderabad are designed to enhance the skin’s appearance by reducing pigmentation issues, including dark spots, redness, or uneven skin tone, leading to a more balanced, glowing complexion.

How does color improvement treatment work?

These treatments work by using advanced technology or topical products that target pigmentation irregularities, stimulating the skin’s natural processes to fade dark spots, even out skin tone, and restore a more uniform appearance.

Are color improvement treatments safe?

Yes, color improvement treatments are generally safe when performed by a certified professional. It’s essential to follow aftercare instructions and discuss your skin type and concerns with a specialist to minimize risks.

How long will it take to see results?

You may begin to see results after 2 to 4 weeks of treatment, but full improvements in skin tone and texture typically appear over a period of 3 to 6 months, depending on the treatment and skin condition.

Is color improvement treatment suitable for all skin types?

Most color improvement treatments are suitable for a range of skin types. However, it's best to consult with a professional to determine the most appropriate solution based on your skin type and concerns.
