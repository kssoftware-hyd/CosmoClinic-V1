How Much Time Does the Direct Hair Transplant Take?

The time required to complete the DHI procedure depends on the number of grafts that need transplanting. On average, it takes between six to eight hours to complete.

Who Is a Suitable Candidate?

Not everyone is an ideal candidate for a DHI hair transplant. During the free analysis, our experts assess it. You are a suitable candidate if you have any of the following characteristics.

  • Men who have hair loss due to male pattern baldness.
  • Women and men who wish to gain voluminous and thick hair.
  • Men who want beard transplants.

Also, to undergo the Direct Hair Transplant (DHI) procedure, you must have healthy hair at the donor site for strong follicle growth. Your hair’s texture, density, and color are considered so you get the desired results.

Specialists at Cosmo International consider your aesthetic goals and various factors, including your medical history and hair loss, to deliver an exceptional service that leaves you with the best results.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is a hair transplant, and how does it work?

A hair transplant is a medical procedure that involves moving hair follicles from one part of the scalp to areas thinning or no hair. At Cosmo Clinic, we offer the best direct hair transplant treatment in Hyderabad using advanced techniques to ensure natural-looking results. This treatment is minimally invasive and performed under local anesthesia.

2. How long does it take to see results from a hair transplant?

Initial results can be seen within 3-6 months, but full results typically appear after 9-12 months. The newly transplanted hair will grow naturally, and you’ll notice improved hair density over time.

3. Why should I choose Cosmo Clinic for my hair transplant in Hyderabad?

We are one of the leading direct hair transplant centers in Hyderabad. We offer advanced hair restoration treatments using the latest technology, ensuring optimal results. Our experienced team of specialists ensures personalized care for every patient.

4. Is the hair transplant procedure painful?

Most patients experience minimal discomfort during the procedure. Local anesthesia is used to numb the scalp, ensuring a pain-free experience. After the procedure, mild soreness or swelling may occur, but it usually resolves within a few days.

5. How do I know if I am a good candidate for a hair transplant?

Ideal candidates are those with thinning or balding areas on their scalp but sufficient donor hair to perform the transplant.
