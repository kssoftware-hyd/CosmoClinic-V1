Overview

Over time, the effects of pollution, sun exposure, and heredity factors show signs of old age on our faces. Increasing age is one of the biggest worries for the human race since ages. However, treatments are available to reverse the signs; one of the best is nonsurgical face lifting. It can help reduce fine lines, lax skin, deep creases, thinning lips, large pores, acne scarring, and sunken cheeks.

Different face-lifting treatments are available, including radiofrequency, HIFU technology, dermal fillers, Botox, thread lift, and laser treatments. At Cosmo International, we provide all the treatments at an affordable price to refresh and rejuvenate your face.

The ideal treatment to suit your needs depends on the condition you have. We recommend you visit us to address the issues with your skin. Our cosmetologists and plastic surgeons will thoroughly understand your concerns and assist you in identifying the treatment to suit your needs.

What is Radiofrequency Skin Rejuvenation?

Radiofrequency treatment, or RF, is an advanced cosmetic treatment for skin rejuvenation that effectively treats large pores, wrinkles, cellulite, age spots, and acne scars and offers skin tightening.

The treatment uses a specially designed device to stimulate collagen and elastin production, reducing the appearance of fine lines and loose skin. It also helps remodel the tissues, reducing fat in the layers of the treated areas.

The RF skincare treatment can provide effective results for skin conditions such as acne management, fine lines and wrinkles, dark circles, and enlarged pores. The facial machines work with high-frequency electrodes for various face and body contours. It is a satisfactory treatment for skin damage comprising burns, acne, and/or cold sores.

How Does Radiofrequency Treatment Work?

The skin treatment uses a handheld device that emits RF waves to heat the skin between 50 to 75 degrees Celsius. It results in the contraction and reshaping of collagen, causing skin tightening. With this treatment, you can expect satisfactory results for tightening both for dermis and epidermis. It is a non-invasive and painless treatment for skin rejuvenation, skin tightening, and body contouring. Consult our experts today if you want to know more about it or start the treatment.

How Does HIFU Treatment Work?

The skin treatment can benefit you for antibacterial action, natural cleaning, and aging skin. The treatment uses focused ultrasound energy to target the layers of the skin just below the surface. It causes the tissue to heat up rapidly, stimulating collagen production, resulting in skin tightening and lifting, removing wrinkles, and minimizing inflamed skin to provide an overall healthier appearance. It is a non-surgical facelift with no downtime and offers long-lasting results. With just one treatment of High-Intensity Focused Ultrasound (HIFU) look 5 to 10 years younger.
