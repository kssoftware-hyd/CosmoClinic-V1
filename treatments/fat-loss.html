What is CoolSculpting?

Also known as Cryolipolysis, CoolSculpting is a non-invasive procedure to contour the body. We perform it using a device to destroy fat cells underneath your body. The sophisticated machine has applicators suitable for different areas.

After applying a protective gel pad, the recommended applicator is used on the targeted fat pocket, grabbing it in its cup to deliver controlled cooling over a specified time. Patients experience sensations of pulling and pinching at the start of the process, but in a few minutes, these sensations subside, as the area becomes numb when complete cooling is achieved.

Overall the procedure involves minimal discomfort, and most of our clients surf the net, read a book, browse their phones, or nap.

Immediately after treatment, the area is massaged to thaw the tissues and further break up the crystallized fatty tissue, and it helps your body to absorb the destroyed fat cells. During this massage, many patients experience some pain, which disappears as soon as the massage ends, and when tissues return to normal temperature.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do fat loss treatments work?

Fat loss treatments work by targeting stubborn fat cells using advanced technology, such as cryolipolysis, laser therapy, or ultrasound. These treatments help reduce fat volume and contour the body by breaking down fat cells over time.

Are fat loss treatments safe?

Yes, non-invasive fat loss treatments are generally safe. However, you should always consult with a professional to ensure the treatment is suitable for your body and health condition, as individual responses may vary.

How long does it take to see results from fat loss treatments?

Results vary but typically begin to show within 3 to 4 weeks after treatment, with the most noticeable changes appearing around 8 to 12 weeks as the body gradually eliminates the targeted fat cells.
