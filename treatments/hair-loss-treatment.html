Overview

Losing a few strands of hair is normal, but massive hair loss can be devastating. You can camouflage a few grey hair strands or ignore a few lines, but losing a chunk of hair can be way more stressful than any other cosmetic issue.

Alopecia is a common condition nowadays, with both men and women suffering from it. Hair loss has various reasons, like stress, hormonal changes, unhealthy diet, heredity, health conditions, and environmental factors. Some are temporary, while some are permanent. If there is a permanent factor causing hair loss, it is vital to look for potential hair loss treatment.

However, there are various hair loss treatments available to combat it. But that’s where the confusion begins.

Cosmo International offers a solution to help those who have been dealing with hair loss and cannot find the proper treatment.

We offer several hair loss treatments, but each one is done according to your condition. Our certified doctors schedule a consultation with you to know your hair care routine, medical and family history, and conduct some tests to find your hair loss cause, and then recommend the treatment.

Non-Surgical Hair Loss Treatments

We offer non-surgical hair loss treatments like PRP and Growth Factor Concentrate (GFC) therapy. These treatments are a painless way to restore your hair. It involves no scarring or downtime and guarantees to give your head a fuller look, diminishing the fact that you have any hair loss issues.

Hair PRP

Platelet Rich Plasma (PRP) therapy for hair loss is a three-step treatment in which the blood is drawn from the patient, processed, and injected into the affected area to stimulate new hair follicles.

  1. Step 1: Our expert draws the blood from the patient’s arm and puts the vial in the centrifuge to separate plasma from the blood.
  2. Step 2: After 10 minutes in the centrifuge, the blood is separated into three layers.
    • Platelet-rich plasma
    • Blood cells
    • Platelet poor plasma
  3. Step 3: The doctors separate the platelet-rich plasma from the layers and inject it into the affected areas of the scalp.

PRP therapy requires a session once a month for 4 to 6 months and maintenance sessions after 4 to 6 months if required. Following this protocol, you can see natural-looking results.

Growth Factor Concentrate (GFC)

Growth factor concentrate therapy is a safe and minimally invasive treatment for hair loss. The concentrated growth factor preparation from the patient’s blood offers superior natural results.

It is a multi-step process where our certified doctors draw the blood from the patient’s arm into GFC tubes and centrifuge it for 10 minutes. The tube is then allowed to stand for 30 minutes which releases the growth factors like vascular endothelial growth factor (VEGF), platelet-derived growth factor (PDGF), epidermal growth factor (EGF), and insulin-like growth factor-1 (IGF-1) from the platelets.

These growth factors are extracted from the platelets and then injected into the affected areas of the scalp for hair regrowth.

GFC therapy is highly effective and promotes natural and enhanced regeneration. However, one requires three sessions for three months to obtain optimum results.

GFC is better than traditional PRP as various studies conducted internationally have shown concentration of stem cell growth factors to be superior in GFC.

Who Are the Best Candidates for PRP and GFC Therapy?

PRP and GFC therapy is effective for both males and females with the early or late signs of Alopecia. However, to ensure you are the best candidate, our doctors must diagnose your scalp, and they will suggest the best possible treatment for your hair loss.

Hair Patches

A hair patch is a quick and easy solution for people suffering from hair loss, especially patchy Alopecia Areata (bald spots). Made with natural human hair and the most realistic-looking materials, you can be assured of the most natural appearance. Moreover, our hair patches are easy to apply and durable, enabling you to go about your daily activities without a care.

You can shower, go to the gym, bathe in a pool, do anything and go where the wind blows.

Our hair patches have a novel technology that absorbs the sweat and natural oil the skin produces, leaving you carefree.

Tape or glue will securely fix your hair patch to your head, giving you a pain-free and non-surgical solution to your hair loss.
