Overview

One of the biggest concerns people have is how to stop the signs of ageing. While it is inevitable, there are solutions to reverse it and have supple and glowing skin.

There are various solutions that effectively boost collagen and elastin production, which helps improve various age-related skin concerns.

Cosmo International offers PRP, carbon facial, HydraFacial, and pixel laser to revive youthfulness, glowing skin, and skin vigor.

What is Skin PRP?

Skin PRP is a facial rejuvenation process that uses the patient’s plasma to stimulate collagen production and cell migration, eliminating fine lines, folds, and wrinkles. PRP is concentrated blood plasma that contains 3 to 5 times the number of platelets found in blood, platelet-derived growth factor (PDGF), transforming growth factor (TGF), vascular endothelial growth factor (VEGF), and other bioactive proteins that help in skin rejuvenation. The results of PRP treatment are natural-looking and subtle and last for two years. It is minimally invasive with no risk of an allergic reaction and can be used to treat any body area without risks of side effects.

What is HydraFacial?

HydraFacial is a four-step facial treatment performed with a proprietary machine that combats dryness and signs of aging, offering you a bright, plump, dewy, and clear complexion. In one session, the treatment cleanses, exfoliates, extracts, and hydrates the skin with serums tailored to your specific concerns, improving skin texture, tone, and appearance. It is a non-invasive treatment with no side effects or downtime, and its effects last longer than other facials at the spa. For maximum results, you must get a HydraFacial treatment every four to six weeks.

What is Pixel Laser?

Whether bothered by fine lines, wrinkles, sun spots, surgical scars, enlarged pores, stretch marks, melasma, rough skin texture, or remnants of acne from your youth, Pixel laser treatment can reverse all these signs offering you younger, healthier-looking skin. Pixel laser resurfacing treatment makes microscopic holes on your skin’s surface through targeted laser energy. The process produces new collagen for firmer, smoother, and youthful skin. Moreover, the treatment is customized to suit your needs and address your unique concerns. It can be done on the face, chest, neck, and other body areas.
